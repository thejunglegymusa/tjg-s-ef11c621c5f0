TJG Signage – playlist editor (prototype, v6)

Every slide is a native template drawn by the player (no websites are embedded). Build a slide below, click Add slide, Preview, then Download and replace playlist.json on the host. Screens pick up changes within refreshMinutes. Wrap unknown text in [[double brackets]] to show it as a highlighted placeholder.

Image/video files must be uploaded to the repo's media/ folder; use paths like media/photo.jpg.
